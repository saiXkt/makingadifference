TAKE ACTION

What can you do?

Helping the environment doesn't always require money. Small actions can contribute to a larger change.

Plant or Protect Trees

Support responsible tree-planting and reforestation projects.

Protect Wildlife

Learn about local wildlife and avoid actions that contribute to habitat destruction.

Spread Awareness

Talk to friends and family about biodiversity, forests and SDG 15.

Reduce Waste

Reuse items, reduce unnecessary consumption and dispose of waste responsibly.
