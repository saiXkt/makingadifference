OUR PURPOSE

Why we built this

This website was created as part of an English 101 presentation to raise awareness about Sustainable Development Goal 15: Life on Land. It explores the threats facing forests, wildlife and ecosystems, shares real data from trusted sources, and invites people to reflect on small actions that support environmental protection.
