SDG 15 Survey

Answer these five questions to help us understand people's views on life on land.

1. How aware were you of deforestation and its impact on ecosystems before visiting this site?

2. Which environmental issue concerns you the most?

3. How concerned are you about the health of forests and ecosystems in your area?

4. Before this, had you ever taken part in an environmental action (tree planting, clean-up, reducing waste, etc.)?

5. Which action are you most willing to commit to after this?
